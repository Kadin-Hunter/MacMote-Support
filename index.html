MacMote — macOS App

Support

Help with setup, troubleshooting your Apple TV bridge connection, or reporting a bug.

01 Getting connected

  • Open MacMote and click the ••• button, or "Add Apple TV…" from the TV menu.
  • Enter the URL of your Apple TV bridge, for example http://apple-tv.local:8080.
  • If your bridge requires a token, add it in the optional field below the URL.
  • Commands only work while your Mac and the bridge are on the same local network.

02 Common issues

  • Buttons don't do anything — check the bridge URL is correct and reachable from a browser on the same Mac.
  • Widget looks blank — remove and re-add the MacMote widget from the widget gallery.
  • Local network permission prompt — approve it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network.

03 Contact

For anything not covered above, reach out and include your macOS version and what you were trying to do.
