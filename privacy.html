MacMote — macOS App

Privacy Policy

Effective August 27, 2026

MacMote is a remote-control app for a paired Apple TV bridge on your local network. It does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data — to us or to anyone else.

01 What MacMote does

MacMote sends button-press commands — navigation, volume, playback — from your Mac to a bridge address you configure yourself, over your local network. That address and an optional access token are the only settings the app stores, and they never leave your Mac.

02 What's stored, and where

The bridge URL and optional access token you enter are saved in a shared UserDefaults app-group container local to your Mac, so the MacMote widget can use the same settings as the main app. This data:

  • Never leaves your device except in the outbound command you send to your own bridge address.
  • Is never transmitted to MacMote's developer or any analytics, advertising, or cloud service.
  • Is removed automatically if you delete the app.

03 Network access

MacMote requests local network access solely to reach the Apple TV bridge address you provide. It does not browse, scan, or connect to any other device or service.

04 Children's privacy

MacMote is a utility app not directed at children and collects no data from any user, regardless of age.

05 Changes to this policy

If MacMote's data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the "effective" date above will reflect the revision.
